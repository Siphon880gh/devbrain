
Requirements: Use ChatGPT app

Open ChatGPT app then press CMD+T
On the right enter your website then ask it to critique the web design and perform browser tasks.

![[Pasted image 20260829000700.png]]

You will notice there's a second mouse icon interacting with the webpage. Not only can it be used to critique or analyze a webpage, it can also browse and interact

Tip: CMD+SHIFT+O to open a new chat thread

If you use **Codex, ChatGPT, or Claude with a 5-hour usage limit** and do most of your intensive coding early in the day, you may exhaust your allowance hours before it resets.

The **"Good Morning" strategy** can help reduce that downtime. By sending a prompt before your workday begins, you start the 5-hour countdown earlier, bringing your next reset closer to the time you typically run out of usage.

### Who Benefits Most?

This strategy is particularly useful for developers who:

1. **Do their heaviest AI coding early in the workday**, exhausting their allowance well before the scheduled reset.
2. **Expect to consume most of their first allowance before the earlier reset**, so little unused capacity goes to waste.

If you rarely exhaust your allowance or you do so on the second allowance of the day, this strategy offers little benefit.

### Example: Starting Work at 7 AM

|Event|Without strategy|With strategy|
|---|---|---|
|First prompt|7:00 AM|5:00 AM|
|Coding begins|7:00 AM|7:00 AM|
|Allowance exhausted|9:30 AM|9:30 AM|
|Allowance resets|12:00 PM|10:00 AM|
|Downtime|2.5 hours|30 minutes|

_Assumes identical coding usage and sufficient allowance until 9:30 AM._

In this example, starting the 5-hour window two hours earlier reduces potential downtime from 2.5 hours to just 30 minutes.

Schedule Codex/Claude to send a quick "good morning" message to intentionally trigger the start of the 5-hour window. In this example, we need the message at 4:30am. The prompt can be as simple as "Good morning". The AI agent will respond back to the greeting, triggering a new 5 hour time frame earlier while you're asleep.

**Key takeaway:** This strategy helps you maximize uninterrupted coding time by aligning your reset with when you're most likely to need additional usage.

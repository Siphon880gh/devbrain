Authority in BI also means doing it correctly:

- Respect **robots.txt** and site Terms of Service
- Prefer **official APIs** (Google CSE, Bing Web Search) where they cover your use case — see [[Providers - Google SERP APIs]]
- Store only what you need; document **data retention** policies
- For EU targets, align collection with **GDPR** lawful basis requirements
- Never scrape credentials, personal data, or paywalled content you are not entitled to access

The goal is durable intelligence, not a cease-and-desist letter.

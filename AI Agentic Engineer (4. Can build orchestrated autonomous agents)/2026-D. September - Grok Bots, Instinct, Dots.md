## Grok Bot

SpaceXAI pushed **Grok Bot** further from an experimental agent into an AI coworker platform. In September, Grok Bot gained **enterprise deployment and governance controls**, while later updates introduced **Team Bots** that can learn from how an organization works. SpaceXAI also demonstrated Bots handling customer support and procurement, and Grok 4.7 was trained to work more naturally within the Bot environment.

## Instinct

**Instinct** continued expanding its autonomous personal-assistant model. A major September change gave each agent **its own email address**, allowing it to create accounts, contact businesses, and follow up on tasks without using the user's inbox. The company also raised **$1 billion at a $10 billion valuation**, while expanding into areas such as travel, shopping, subscriptions, and phone-based concierge tasks.

It's trained to use a phone and a computer. You can text or call it. It uses its devices in the same way that humans do.

## OpenAI Dots

OpenAI entered the always-on personal-agent race on **September 29** with **Dots**. Unlike a normal chatbot waiting for prompts, a Dot can continue working autonomously in the cloud, navigate websites and applications, manage longer-running projects, and interact through services such as ChatGPT, Slack, and Teams. Dots launched initially for higher-tier business and Pro users and compete directly with products such as Grok Bot, Instinct, and Meta's Muse.

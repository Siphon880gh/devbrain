Marina trained an AI model and loaded it into an ESP 32 device:

Marina designed and 3D printed a smart AI food scale that uses an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano and a custom-trained vision model to recognize food. By combining food recognition with real-time weight measurements, it estimates calories, protein, carbs, and fat on a touchscreen and tracks multiple foods as they are added to a plate.
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D9Sbp8rlH7I&t=136s

Tina Huang built a Raspberry Pi AI assistant that chains together several models to listen and respond in speech using Whisper, Qwen 3.1 7B and Piper Text-to-Speech

The video’s rough calculation is:

**Model size in billions ≈ (RAM in GB × 0.75) ÷ 0.6**

For example: **32 GB × 0.75 ÷ 0.6 = 40B**.

Treat these as the presenter’s examples and rough capacity estimates, **not guaranteed usable limits or speeds**. The formula assumes a particular compressed model footprint; model format, context length, and runtime memory also matter.

PrettyPrompt is great at expanding your prompt, covering details you missed, and understanding your intention. However, it tends to turn prompts into long essays with bullet points and multiple paragraphs. If you save many rewritten prompts, this makes them harder to scan and understand later.

The trick is to rewrite your prompt with PrettyPrompt first, then paste the result into this prompt to make it more condensed and easier for the human to read:

```text
Condense this down to a short prompt:

Prompt:
'''
{YOUR REWRITTEN PROMPT FROM PRETTYPROMPT}
'''
```

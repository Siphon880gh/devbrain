**What it is:** Superpower ChatGPT is a Chrome extension (also on Edge/Brave) that layers a management workspace on top of the standard ChatGPT interface. It holds a 4.5-star rating with 3,600+ reviews on the Chrome Web Store and was one of the first ChatGPT extensions released.<sup data-citation="1"></sup><sup data-citation="2"></sup>

**Key features:**
- **Folders & subfolders** — organize chats into a custom hierarchy instead of one endless sidebar list
- **Full-text search** — find any past conversation instantly, plus timestamps and a minimap for long threads
- **Prompt management** — a personal prompt library, prompt chains, and community prompts
- **Export tools** — bulk export or delete chats; save individual conversations as PDF, Markdown, or JSON
- **Extras** — notes and highlights on messages, image gallery for generated images, auto-archiving of old chats, and Showcase sharing<sup data-citation="1"></sup><sup data-citation="2"></sup><sup data-citation="4"></sup>

**Why use it:**

1. **ChatGPT's native sidebar doesn't scale.** Once you have hundreds of chats, finding an old conversation is painful. Folders plus fast search solve this directly — the most-praised benefit in user reviews.<sup data-citation="2"></sup>
2. **It stops prompt rework.** A saved prompt library means your best prompts are reusable assets rather than text you retype or hunt for.
3. **Your data becomes portable.** Bulk export to Markdown/JSON/PDF gives you backups and lets you move conversations outside OpenAI's silo.<sup data-citation="2"></sup>
4. **Zero friction.** It installs in one click and integrates natively into ChatGPT's UI — no separate app or setup.<sup data-citation="3"></sup>

**Caveat worth knowing:** users with very large chat histories (300+ chats) report that moving chats into folders one at a time is tedious, since bulk-organization options are limited.<sup data-citation="3"></sup>

**Bottom line:** if you use ChatGPT casually, you don't need it. If you use it daily for work — juggling many projects, prompts, and conversations — it fills the organizational gaps OpenAI hasn't addressed, and the core features are free.
